# Bibliography

Consolidated academic references for the Mandarin Syntax Explorer project. These works inform the Fractal Logic Stream (FLS) framework, ghost node conventions, and pedagogical design decisions documented throughout this repository.

---

## Modern Chinese Linguistics

Chao, Y.R. (1968). *A Grammar of Spoken Chinese*. Berkeley: University of California Press.

Huang, Chu-Ren & Dingxu Shi (Eds.). (2016). *A Reference Grammar of Chinese*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

Li, C.N. & Thompson, S.A. (1981). *Mandarin Chinese: A Functional Reference Grammar*. Berkeley: University of California Press.

Liu Yuehua 刘月华 et al. (2001). *实用现代汉语语法* [Practical Modern Chinese Grammar]. Beijing: Commercial Press.

Lü Shuxiang 吕叔湘 (1980/1999). *现代汉语八百词* [800 Words of Modern Chinese]. Beijing: Commercial Press.

Paul, W. (2015). *New Perspectives on Chinese Syntax*. Berlin: De Gruyter Mouton.

Paul, W. & Whitman, J. (2017). "Topic Prominence." In *The Blackwell Companion to Syntax*, 2nd ed., Vol. VII, ch. 117, pp. 4473–4503. Oxford: Wiley-Blackwell.

Zhu Dexi 朱德熙 (1982). *语法讲义* [Lectures on Grammar]. Beijing: Commercial Press.

## Classical Chinese Linguistics

Aldridge, E. (2013). "Survey of Chinese Historical Syntax: Pre-Archaic and Archaic Chinese." *Language and Linguistics Compass*, 7(1), 58–77.

Ma Jianzhong 马建忠 (1898). *马氏文通* [Ma's Grammar]. Shanghai.

Meisterernst, B. (Ed.). (2016). *New Aspects of Classical Chinese Grammar*. Wiesbaden: Harrassowitz.

Peyraube, A. (1996). "Recent Issues in Chinese Historical Syntax." In C.-T.J. Huang & Y.-H.A. Li (Eds.), *New Horizons in Chinese Linguistics* (pp. 161–213). Dordrecht: Kluwer.

Pulleyblank, E.G. (1995). *Outline of Classical Chinese Grammar*. Vancouver: UBC Press.

Wang Li 王力 (1962–1964). *古代汉语* [Ancient Chinese]. 4 vols. Beijing: Zhonghua Book Company.

## General Linguistics / Typology

Bisang, W. (2020). "Grammaticalization in Chinese — A Cross-Linguistic Perspective." In J.Z. Xing (Ed.), *A Typological Approach to Grammaticalization and Lexicalization: East Meets West* (pp. 17–54). Berlin: De Gruyter Mouton.

Chomsky, N. (1981). *Lectures on Government and Binding*. Dordrecht: Foris.

Huang, C.T.J. (1984). "On the Distribution and Reference of Empty Pronouns." *Linguistic Inquiry*, 15(4), 531–574.

Huang, C.-T.J. (2015). "On Syntactic Analyticity and Parametric Theory." In A. Li, A. Simpson & W.-T.D. Tsai (Eds.), *Chinese Syntax in a Cross-Linguistic Perspective* (pp. 1–48). Oxford: Oxford University Press.

Huang, C.-T.J., Li, Y.-H.A. & Simpson, A. (Eds.). (2014). *The Handbook of Chinese Linguistics*. Oxford: Wiley-Blackwell.

Li, C.N. & Thompson, S.A. (1976). "Subject and Topic: A New Typology of Language." In C.N. Li (Ed.), *Subject and Topic* (pp. 457–489). New York: Academic Press.

Rizzi, L. (1986). "Null Objects in Italian and the Theory of *pro*." *Linguistic Inquiry*, 17(3), 501–557.

Tsao, F.-F. (1990). *Sentence and Clause Structure in Chinese: A Functional Perspective*. Taipei: Student Book Co.

## Pedagogical Grammar / L2 Teaching

Fisac, T. & Moratto, R. (2024). *Fundamental Structures of the Chinese Language: Topic-Comment and Other Key Structures*. London: Routledge.

Yuan, F., He, B. & Hu, W. (Eds.). (2022). *Pedagogical Grammar and Grammar Pedagogy in Chinese as a Second Language*. London: Routledge.
